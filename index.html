The problem

Every commute comes with too many unknowns.

Transportation information exists — it's just scattered across too many places to compare easily before you leave.

Uncertain waiting times

Traffic delays

Confusing options

Different prices, same trip

Crowded, uncomfortable rides

The solution

One place. Every option.

Mwasalaty brings buses, microbuses, metro, and shared rides together so you can compare your options before you leave.
